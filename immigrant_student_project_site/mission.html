Our mission

Making space for
the whole story.

We believe students should not have to choose between their cultures, their families, their identities, and their own wellbeing.

What we hope to do

Start conversations that are often left unspoken.

Rooted & Rising is a student-led project focused on the mental-health experiences of younger students from immigrant families. The goal is to make information easier to find, experiences easier to talk about, and support easier to understand.

We want this to be a place where students can learn without feeling judged and where their experiences are taken seriously.

Clear, accessible information about mental health and the experiences students may face.
